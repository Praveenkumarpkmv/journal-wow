Issue 01

The Audacity to build.

Our inaugural issue revolves around the theme of Audacity. The mindset of mavericks. The courage to step away from the expected, to follow a path of your own, and to venture where few have gone before.

A Journal On Audacity includes interviews and insights from Volvo, Nåva Space, Omnipollo, Nothing, ICA, Vipps, 1000 Skis, SJ, Sparebank 1, Letters from Sweden, Industrifonden. And many more.

The Mindset

What does it take to step away from the expected?

“To follow a path of your own, and to venture where few have gone before. Audacity isn't loud. It's the quiet decision to ignore the noise and build the thing that doesn't exist yet.”

This is a call for dialogue around exceptional products and brands. To reflect and challenge ourselves, we've talked to the ones who got it right, gathered exclusive insights, and put them on paper.
